Allow camera access

CinePilot's Face Pause needs your webcam to tell when you're looking at the screen. Everything is processed on your device — no video, images, or face data ever leave your computer or get saved.

Granting it here lets the detector run quietly in the background, so it keeps working in fullscreen and never interrupts a video to ask. Auto Fullscreen works without the camera, so you can skip this and still use that half of the extension.

Camera blocked? How to fix
  1. Click the camera / lock icon in the address bar of this page.
  2. Set Camera to Allow.
  3. Click Enable camera again.
