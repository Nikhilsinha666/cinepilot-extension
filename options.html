Site lists

One domain per line. Subdomains are covered automatically, so reddit.com also blocks old.reddit.com.

Allowlist

Stays reachable while Hyper Focus is on. Put the things you genuinely need next to a video here, like your notes or docs.

Blocklist

Blocked at all times, whether Hyper Focus is on or not. This is for the sites you never want to end up on by reflex.

chrome:// pages are never blocked, so you can always reach your settings and turn any of this off.
