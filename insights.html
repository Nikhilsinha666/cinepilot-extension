What is and is not measured

Focus is reported as a share of measured time, not of watch time. Attention is only knowable while Face Pause is on and a YouTube window is in front, because that is when the camera is running. Time watched with the camera off still counts as watched, but is deliberately excluded from the percentage rather than guessed at.

Only these counters are stored: milliseconds watched, measured, focused and away, time in Hyper Focus, and how many times each feature paused you. No video frames, images, face data, titles or URLs are recorded.
